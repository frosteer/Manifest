Add your ultimate goal on the left panel.
Add the main task cards related to your ultimate goal in your middle timeline section.
Add sub-tasks to break down the main task cards into smaller steps.

You can save your perfect day to repeat all the main task cards and the respective sub-task cards every day.
You can then manually adjust your perfect day to fit your day.
This makes sure you prioritize the main task cards that will help you achieve your ultimate goals, while also giving you the flexibility to adjust your day to different events in life.

The goal of this app is to help you stack enough moments in life, observe your life, and turn your vision into reality.
Hence, the name of this app is Manifest.

The concept is simple.
If you stack enough right moments in your life, your vision will become a reality.

For example, if your ultimate goal is to have a tree,
you only need to plant the seed in the soil and make sure it receives sufficient water and sunlight every day for it to grow into a tree.
Planting the seed and feeding it with sufficient water and sunlight are the stacking moments that need to happen.
The more it happens, the higher the chance for you to get the result you want, which is the tree and also the fruit it bears.

This is the same in life.
Everything you want to own in life requires energy to manifest.
The more efficient, right energy you put into it, the higher the chance that energy will transform into the reality you want, in the form you want.

Seven Sequential Steps of Manifestation

Direct from the Bashar Library vault: Bashar's August 1995 Session discussing the seven sequential steps of manifestation.


Step 1: Vision

Visualize what you want.


Step 2: Desire

Be intensely excited about what you are visualizing.


Step 3: Belief

Believe what you desire is possible to manifest.
 

Step 4: Acceptance

Accept your belief and your ability to manifest it as being true.


Step 5: Intent

Want and intend are different. You must have the intention to manifest your desire.


Step 6: Action

Act and behave like your desire has already manifested.


Step 7: Allowance

Detach from the outcome. You have to be intense in your desire without any expectation that it manifests.
